## Slide 4: SLM vs LLM - Quick Comparison

### When to Choose SLM:

✅ High volume, specific tasks  
✅ Data privacy is critical  
✅ Budget constraints  
✅ Low-latency requirements  
✅ Offline/edge deployment needed  

### When LLM is Better:

⚠️ Complex reasoning required  
⚠️ General-purpose assistant  
⚠️ Low volume (<10K queries/month)  
⚠️ Rapid prototyping
